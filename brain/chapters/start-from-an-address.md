# Start from an address

Any address can be the start: a person's wallet, a contract, a DAO, a token.

1. **What is it?** `look_up` with the address. For an EVM address, `read_chain` says whether it's a contract or an account, what it answers, and its verified source. A contract that answers `sharesToken()` and `lootToken()` is a Moloch v3 DAO; the routes name the recipe for each kind Petals knows.
2. **Where does it already sit on the map?** `account_groups` lists every stored group it appears in, with its share, rank and date. Say these as groups: "among the holders of X, the stakers of Y".
3. **Say what you found in one or two lines,** then offer where to go: the group it matters most in, what it holds or did that isn't mapped yet, or, for a contract, the power inside it (`where-power-lives`).

If it's a kind of contract no recipe knows, that's `a-new-system`.
