# Money

What holdings are worth, when they unlock, and what a treasury paid out.

## When to use which chapter

| Chapter | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `value` | estimating the value of identified holdings and separating price, backing and spending power |
| `vesting` | finding when an allocation becomes available and who controls its release or claim rights |
| `payouts` | what a treasury paid out over a period, to whom, and what it was worth on the day it was paid |
