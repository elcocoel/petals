# Full DAO report

A free service for Petals' agent or the person's own agent. Combine the eight services into one document with a shared scope and evidence record.

**Reads:** `brain_context` for the DAO question and doctrine, then `brain_file` for the service chapters named in `continues`. Use their named tools and data sources; `search_petals` resolves the DAO. Reuse the same readings, ballots and price observations across sections.

1. **Set the scope once.** Record DAO, chain/contracts, audience, as-of date, proposal period, source dates and coverage. Use the requested proposal, delegate and comparison target where supplied; do not choose arbitrary ones to fill space.
2. **Run the applicable chapters.** Preserve their distinctions between holdings, delegated power, ballots, authority, treasury value and release rights. Use the proposal-writing chapter only when there is a particular person's evidenced contribution to match.
3. **Assemble this template.** Fill each supported section; for unavailable or inapplicable sections, give one line explaining why.

## Report template

- **Scope and findings:** DAO identity, observation bounds and the supported findings that answer the person's question.
- **Distribution card:** Matching flower, measure, concentration figures, date and coverage.
- **Governance health:** How concentrated power is, how few reach the stated threshold, turnout, delegation and evidenced voting blocs.
- **Proposal brief:** Requested proposal, recorded choices, bloc context, requested money and evidenced execution.
- **Delegate scorecard:** Requested account, dated delegated power, participation and ballot evidence.
- **Treasury and unlocks:** Recorded assets, valuation basis, payouts from this treasury over the stated period via `list_payouts`, and chronological conditional releases.
- **Authority map:** Who chooses, approves, blocks and executes spending or rule changes; unread permission paths.
- **Comparison:** Requested peer or earlier period, compatible figures and supported differences.
- **A contribution that fits:** Sourced current DAO need, this person's relevant evidence and the unsent proposal draft, if a match exists.
- **Evidence and limits:** Source links, reading hashes/blocks where available, figure ids, calculation inputs, bloc method/thresholds, price times and missing coverage. Mark inferences and leads.

**Missing data:** Combining sections adds no ballot coverage, historical readings, permissions, vesting schedules or personal context; carry each gap into the report.

**The person gets:** One report using this template, with a single evidence record and no invented completeness score.
