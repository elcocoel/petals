# Governance

Who holds power, who votes, who moves money and who changes rules.

## When to use which chapter

| Chapter | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `where-power-lives` | finding where power sits in any on-chain system, and reading it with a shape |
| `who-gets-a-vote` | distinguishing holdings, membership, delegation and the right to vote, join or leave |
| `from-proposal-to-action` | following a proposal from eligibility through voting to execution |
| `who-can-move-money-or-change-rules` | tracing treasury execution and authority to change permissions or contract rules |
| `voting-blocs-over-time` | comparing actual ballots to find groups that keep voting alike over a stated period |
| `stake-behind-the-network` | separating validator consensus power from the accounts and stake behind it |
| `moloch-v3` (recipe) | A Moloch v3 DAO (Baal, as DAOhaus deploys it): who holds its shares and loot, and what they did in it |
