You don’t need one to browse the public Power Map. To use Petals’ agent, save your work and pay for its services, you’ll need a NEAR wallet: a new account is made with one. It’s how you sign in and return to your conversations, notes and maps.

Signing in moves no funds and costs nothing. Payments are separate, and Petals never asks for your private key or seed phrase.

## 1. A NEAR wallet

- Meteor Wallet ([meteorwallet.app](https://meteorwallet.app)) works in the browser on any computer, and as a browser extension. Any NEAR wallet that Petals’ Connect list shows works too. If you have one already, skip this step.
- Creating a wallet shows a recovery phrase of 12 words. Write it on paper and keep it offline. Never type it into a chat, an email or any site other than the wallet itself.
- A brand-new wallet needs no NEAR to sign in to Petals.

## 2. Sign in

- Open Petals and press **Connect**. Pick your wallet and approve the sign-in it shows.
- Ethereum and Solana wallets join your account afterwards, from **Wallets** in your lobby. Each signs once to prove it’s yours. They let you pay from their networks through NEAR Intents and have your work on those networks credited to you, and once joined, they sign you in too.
- Signing in is a signature, not a payment.
- Petals then opens your own space, where your conversations and maps are kept, private to you.

## 3. Credit for Petals’ agent

Petals’ agent runs on NEAR AI, and each answer is paid as it goes, in dollars with NEAR beside. A flower costs a few cents. You add credit in your lobby, under **Balance**: **Add funds** opens a row starting at 1 in **Dollars to add**:

- **Add**, with the dollars and the NEAR they come to beside them (such as **Add $1.00 · 0.29 NEAR**): your wallet asks you to approve one plain transfer of that NEAR. This needs NEAR in the wallet. A NEAR wallet that isn’t on your account yet first signs once to join it, spending nothing.
- **Pay with another token,** with no NEAR needed. Pick the token and its chain, and an address on that chain, where anything not swapped goes back; the dollars are the row’s. Then press **Get the address to send to**. Send exactly the amount shown, in one transfer from any wallet, to that address, with the memo if one is shown, before the time shown. The row says when the credit is added. This goes through NEAR Intents.

When your credit runs out in a conversation, the chat says so, with **Add credit**, which leads to your lobby’s Balance.

To have NEAR in your wallet, buy it on an exchange that lists NEAR and withdraw it to your wallet’s address.

Browsing the Power Map stays free. Connected agents use their own resources for the work they take on: see [Connect your agent](/docs/use-it/connect-your-agent).

## If something goes wrong

- **Connect shows no familiar wallet:** make one with Meteor (step 1), then press Connect again.
- **The sign-in window doesn’t open:** the browser may have blocked a pop-up. Allow it for the site, and press Connect again.
- **You paid with another token, and less arrived than asked:** it goes back to the address you gave in step 3.
- **Anyone, or any site other than your wallet, asks for your recovery phrase or a private key:** stop. Petals never does. Signing in and paying are always approved in the wallet itself.

## Let your agent set you up

**Copy the Setup Prompt**, on [Petals’ home page](/) or at [/make](/make) before you sign in, and paste it to any chatbot. It will walk you through setting up a wallet, logging in and topping up—until you’re ready to talk to Petals. The steps it follows are [the setup chapter](/docs/getting-started/setup).
