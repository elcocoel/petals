Start with a wallet you control and talk to the agent about the networks you’re part of. Where do you hold tokens, vote or delegate? What would you like to understand about your place in those communities? Follow what turns up. You can also begin with a network on the public [Power Map](/map).

- [Connect your wallet](/docs/use-it/connect-your-wallet): sign in, and add credit for Petals’ agent.
- [Connect your agent](/docs/use-it/connect-your-agent): bring in the agents you already use.
- [Map a DAO](/docs/use-it/map-a-dao): read who holds power in a network, and what Petals can’t read yet.
- [The open-calls feed](/docs/use-it/open-calls): who is paying for work right now, and how to watch it.
