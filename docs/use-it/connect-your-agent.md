Petals can outsource the heavy mapping and research to agents you pay for. They work with their own tools and resources, bringing results back while you keep exploring with Petals.

Claude Code, Codex, Hermes and other agents that can run commands can help you map. Copy the connection prompt into your agent to bring it into your Petals project.

Petals’ agent works with you on the page and can hand mapping jobs to your connected agents. They use their own tools and resources, and bring the results back into the same project. Several agents can work on different parts of a network, guided by the shared Brain.

ChatGPT and the Claude chat app can also connect through Petals’ connector. Your app may ask you to approve the connection or particular actions.

None of this is needed to start: Petals’ agent works on its own once you’ve [connected your wallet](/docs/use-it/connect-your-wallet).

## Connect one

Sign in, then press **Connect an agent** at the top of Petals’ side panel. It copies the prompt for your agent. Paste it into your agent’s chat.

Each app has its own chapter, with the steps your agent follows:

| Your app                        | Where it runs                                              | Its chapter                                                         |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Claude Code                     | In a terminal, or under **Code** in the Claude desktop app | [Connect Claude Code](/docs/connect-your-agent/connect-claude-code) |
| Codex                           | In a terminal                                              | [Connect Codex](/docs/connect-your-agent/connect-codex)             |
| Hermes                          | In a terminal, or over ACP                                 | [Connect Hermes](/docs/connect-your-agent/connect-hermes)           |
| The Claude app, ChatGPT, Cursor | In the cloud, as a connector at Petals’ `/mcp` address     | [Connect a cloud app](/docs/connect-your-agent/connect-cloud)       |

## On your computer or in the cloud

**An agent on your computer** (Claude Code, Codex or Hermes) runs Petals’ tools there. It can look up a new name or address, read a chain and search the web.

**A cloud app** (the Claude app, ChatGPT) connects to Petals’ server instead. It reads what Petals already holds: networks, stored readings, flowers and relations. It counts and draws from those readings, keeps a conversation on Petals’ page, takes jobs and reads the brain. It can’t make a fresh reading, and it takes jobs when you ask it to rather than waiting for them.

## Jobs

Once connected, your agent waits for jobs from Petals’ agent and brings what it finds back into the project the job came from. When a job is big enough, an app that has sub-agents of its own can split it among them and report it once, for all of them.

Your connected agents show in Petals’ side panel, with what each is doing.
