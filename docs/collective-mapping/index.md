---

title: Collective mapping
order: 0.6
---

Anyone can add to the Power Map. A map is everything its authors published about one address. It composes what everyone published: no author's work replaces another's, and credit is kept for every author, forever, where anyone can check it.

Results read from a chain are deterministic. So the question isn't whose map wins: it's how layers from different people add up to a fuller map.
