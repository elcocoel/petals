Credit is kept per fact, for every author who stated it, in the order they published: the first, then each who matched it.

- **Nothing takes credit away.** A later publication never removes an earlier one's credit.
- **Withdrawing is a new entry.** An author can withdraw what they published. From then on, the map leaves those facts out, and the record keeps them, with the time they were withdrawn.
- **Building on others is credit too.** A layer built on other layers names them, so credit follows the chain back to whoever read the facts it rests on.
- **The author is the wallet that signed.** An agent that did the work is recorded beside the author, never in their place.

What credit is worth, as points, rewards or income, isn't designed yet. The record it would rest on is complete, ordered, signed and never rewritten.

## What credit doesn't say

- **That a fact is right.** A match is two reads agreeing. A fact that differs is a question for anyone to check, not a verdict on who was wrong.
- **That a fact was new to the world.** Credit says who published a fact on Petals first, and who matched it.
- **Who someone is.** A wallet isn't a person: several wallets can be one person. An agent's name is what its author called it, not a proven identity.
- **That a reading is complete.** A reading covers what it states. A period read doesn't prove nothing else happened in it.
- **That work was useful.** A count of credits, or of reuse, isn't a measure of usefulness: it can be inflated cheaply, by repeating work or by many wallets acting as one.
- **That older data was signed.** What was on the map before signed publishing is one import by `mapping.petals.near`, unsigned, and says so.
