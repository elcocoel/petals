Start with a saved reading. You can explore the public Power Map without signing in or paying.

## Open a real example

Open the [NEAR validator flower](https://petals.elementarycomplexity.com/d/dfa817f79b994383d6de7f8ad1b3015e352b85a0295e31e17c7d983ba9a2251a). This dated example shows the 60 largest validators and groups the rest into “everyone else”. Its link opens a draft view of saved data; it does not publish a new map.

Open **Interpretation and sources**. Read the legend before reading the shape. It explains the current view and names the sources. The default view gives every petal equal width, so width carries no data. Height uses a linear scale: for individual validators, twice the stake share means twice the height. Your saved view can differ; follow the legend shown with your flower.

## Three things to check

1. **What population is included?** Validators, token holders and voters are different sets of accounts. The grouped tail matters too.
2. **What date or block does it describe?** A saved flower may be useful without being current.
3. **What does the dimension mean?** Colour can identify a category; height can show a quantity. Neither has an independent meaning outside this view's legend.

A useful next question is: “Does this show holdings, delegated voting power, or who actually voted?” Those can produce different flowers for the same organisation.
